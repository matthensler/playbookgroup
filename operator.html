FAQ

Common questions.

Is this a franchise or employment role? +
Neither. You operate as an independent operator — not a franchisee and not an employee. You own your business activity within the Playbook Group platform and earn revenue based on what you produce.
Do I need prior event or marketing experience? +
Prior experience is helpful but not required. Playbook Group provides operating playbooks, approved event formats, and marketing offerings ready to sell. You bring the drive and local market knowledge.
Can I participate in both events and marketing? +
Yes — and that's the model. Combining events and marketing services is what creates a diversified, growing business. Both streams are included in your operator access.
Do I have to participate in both events and marketing? +
No. You choose where to start. Some operators lead with events, others with marketing. You decide how to build your business — the platform supports both paths.
Who controls pricing and contracts? +
Playbook Group controls pricing and contracts. Operators do not set their own pricing or manage contracts independently. This structure is what allows us to maintain brand standards and support you without adding operational burden.
When do I get paid? +
Revenue is tracked by Playbook Group and paid to operators after funds have cleared. Specific payout schedules are outlined in your operator agreement.
Is there a long-term commitment? +
No long-term commitment is required beyond the monthly $29 operator fee. City approvals are granted on a rolling basis and subject to availability and performance.
